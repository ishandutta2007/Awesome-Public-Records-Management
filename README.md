# Awesome-Public-Records-Management

## Top Public Records Management Platforms Ecosystem

**Curated List of SaaS Products & Open-Source GitHub Projects**

*Focused on FOIA/Public Records Requests, Records Retention, Document Management & Transparency Portals*

**Last updated: September 2026**



This repository tracks notable **SaaS platforms** and **open-source projects** for **Public Records Management**. These systems help government agencies receive, track, fulfill, and report on public records / FOIA requests while managing underlying document repositories and retention policies.



**Examples** include GovQA, JustFOIA, NextRequest, CivicPlus Records, OpenCities Records, Hyland OnBase, Laserfiche, OpenText Content Suite, M-Files, and DocuWare (the category leaders).



**Open-source emphasis**: Specialized FOIA request portals are largely commercial. Strong open options exist in enterprise document/content management (**Mayan EDMS**, Alfresco community, Paperless-ngx, and related systems) that can support records repositories and workflows. This section highlights practical open alternatives and is realistic about the gap in dedicated request-tracking software.



Contributions welcome! Open a PR to add/update entries. Keep descriptions factual and link to official sites.

## SaaS/Hosted Platforms

- **[GovQA (Granicus)](https://granicus.com/solution/govqa/)**  

  Widely used public records / FOIA request management platform for large cities and government agencies.



- **[JustFOIA](https://www.justfoia.com/)**  

  Public records request management software designed to simplify intake, tracking, fulfillment, and reporting for state and local governments.



- **[NextRequest (CivicPlus)](https://www.civicplus.com/)**  

  Modern FOIA and public records request portal that centralizes the request lifecycle for government agencies.



- **[CivicPlus Records](https://www.civicplus.com/)**  

  Records and request management solutions within the broader CivicPlus government technology suite.



- **[OpenCities Records](https://www.opencities.com/)** (or similar civic platforms)  

  Digital government platforms that include public records and transparency features.



- **[Hyland OnBase](https://www.hyland.com/)**  

  Enterprise content services and case management platform frequently used for government records and document workflows.



- **[Laserfiche](https://www.laserfiche.com/)**  

  Enterprise content management and process automation platform with strong records management capabilities.



- **[OpenText Content Suite](https://www.opentext.com/)**  

  Enterprise information management platform used for large-scale records and content governance.



- **[M-Files](https://www.m-files.com/)**  

  Metadata-driven document and records management platform supporting compliance and information governance.



- **[DocuWare](https://www.docuware.com/)**  

  Document management and workflow platform used for records capture, storage, and process automation.

### Additional Strong Open-Source Options

- Using **Mayan EDMS** or **Alfresco Community** as the core records repository with strong access control and audit trails.

- Deploying **Paperless-ngx** for lighter-weight document capture and archiving needs.

- Building or adapting open request-tracking workflows on top of an open ECM for smaller agencies.

- Combining open document management with commercial FOIA portals when specialized request handling is required.

- Accepting that purpose-built FOIA intake portals, automated redaction at scale, multi-department collaboration features, and government-specific compliance reporting still favor commercial platforms (GovQA, JustFOIA, NextRequest, etc.).



**Frameworks for building custom systems**: Deploy an open ECM (Mayan EDMS or Alfresco) as the records repository → implement request intake and tracking via custom or low-code workflows → apply retention policies and audit logging → publish status or responsive documents through a public portal. This approach gives strong control over documents and can work for agencies with technical capacity. Commercial FOIA and records platforms remain the practical choice for most governments that need proven request lifecycle management, citizen-facing portals, and vendor support.
